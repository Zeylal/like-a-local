Day by day 📔

Day 1 · Sat 15 — Benvenuti a Milano

Landed 12:10 → Malpensa Express to Centrale (~50 min) → dropped bags at the studio in Porta Venezia.

Afternoon: Duomo rooftop terraces (walk among the spires!), Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, cobbled lanes of Brera.

Evening: aperitivo on the Navigli — one spritz comes with a little buffet. The city is empty for Ferragosto, in the best way.

Day 2 · Sun 16 — Lake Como, Varenna 💙

Train from Centrale to Varenna-Esino (~1h10, ~7 € — no booking needed).

Pastel village, the lakeside passerella walk, Villa Monastero gardens, a swim at the lido, gelato with lake view.

Option we loved: the 15-min ferry across to Bellagio for the afternoon.

Day 3 · Mon 17 — Verona, city of Romeo & Juliet

Regional train ~1h50. The Roman Arena, Piazza delle Erbe, Juliet's balcony, Ponte Pietra, sunset from Castel San Pietro.

The big one: open-air opera inside the 2,000-year-old Arena — stone-step tickets from ~30 €, bring a cushion.

Alt on the way: hop off at Peschiera for Sirmione, the castle-village on Lake Garda.

Day 4 · Tue 18 — Ciao, Milano

Slow morning at Castello Sforzesco & Parco Sempione, one last espresso + brioche, then the 17:00 flight home.

The route 🗺

Budget & tips 💶

  • Flights: 189 € pp return (Orly→Malpensa / Malpensa→CDG, direct).
  • Stay: 338 € for 3 nights — 35 m² studio, AC, kitchen, self check-in, Porta Venezia.
  • Trains: day trips are fixed-price regionale — Varenna ~7 €, Verona ~14 € each way. Buy on the Trenord/Trenitalia app, no need to book ahead. Validate paper tickets!
  • Malpensa Express: ~13 € each way, ~50 min from Centrale.
  • Ferragosto (Aug 15): some family restaurants close, but tourist areas, lakes & Verona stay open — and hotel prices drop. Best-value week for Milan.
  • Heat: ~33 °C in August — AC non-negotiable, swimsuits for Como.
